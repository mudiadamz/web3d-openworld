Keys

Moving
WASDmove — flies, walks or slides the rig, depending on the view
QEdown and up (fly, orbit)
shiftsprint. There is one move speed and it is not adjustable — it is set by DAY_LENGTH, because a short day is a fast one
draglook around — orbits the pivot in Orbit, circles the person in Follow
scrollzoom in Orbit · how far back you stand in Follow
Views Fly
Cnext view — Fly, Walk, Orbit, Follow
Ffollow somebody — again for somebody else. Their energy is on the caption, because it is what decides what they take on
Rsomewhere else on the island — drops you into Orbit over a random spot, and again for another one. Dry land and not a cliff, so there is always something under you
click(in Follow) go there — click the ground and the person you are behind walks to it, on their own, with a ring on the spot. Click again and they turn round. Nothing else steers them while you have them — not dusk, not a job, not a tiger — but they still tire, still get hungry, and can still be caught
W(in Follow) hold to make them run instead of walk. It is a jog and it is charged for like one — it costs energy, it slows with a full basket, and it gives out when there is nothing left
shift+W(in Follow) let go, and they pick up their own life where they stand
icons(in Follow) a row across the bottom tells them what to do rather than where to go — find food, hunt, knap, tend the fire, rest, walk to the next band. One instruction, taken up once: afterwards they are choosing for themselves again
On screen
Hthe world panel
Mthe map, one size bigger — small, medium, large, xlarge, hidden, and round again. shift+M goes back the other way. Click it to travel there
[]slower and faster — the clock, and everything living on it. Once everybody is in, the night is run through rather than watched and takes about a second; NIGHT_SKIP_RATE is how much of each frame is spent doing it
Bhide one more layer — press until something stops looking wrong, and the last name shown is the culprit. shift+B puts it all back
Tthe band you are watching — who they are, their ages, how many children, what each of them has carried home. Click a name to follow them, which is how you reach somebody who is indoors or over a hill
Lthe whole chronicle — every line from every world, searchable. It opens on what is worth telling: what a band worked out, who broke away and who went with them, who took somebody in, the days the store ran out. The button beside it gives back every kill and birth as well

Everything else is configured in .env. Your place is kept across reloads and server restarts; ?fresh=1 ignores it. From the address bar: ?models=off and ?quality=low if it runs slow. When something looks wrong, turn one thing off at a time: ?shadows=0, ?water=0, ?streams=0, ?grass=0, ?flowers=0, ?wind=0. Hills shading each other is off because it’s the one thing that speckled — ?terrainshadow=1 brings it back.
